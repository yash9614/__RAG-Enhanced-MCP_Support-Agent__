## Sample FAQ

### How do I reset my password?
Go to the login page and click "Forgot password". You will receive an email with a reset link.

### Certificate generation
Certificates are issued by admins after course completion. Support agents can check status but cannot issue them.

### Refund policy
Refunds are available within 7 days of purchase if less than 20% of the course has been completed.
